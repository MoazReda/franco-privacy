Privacy Policy — Franco

Last updated: September 2026

Franco ("the app") is an offline Egyptian Franco-Arabic transliteration app. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Collect

Franco does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely on your device (offline). We do not have any servers, and no information ever leaves your phone.

How Translation Works

All translation happens locally on your device using an on-device model. The text you enter is processed on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Translation History

If you use the history feature, your past translations are stored only on your own device. They are never uploaded or shared, and you can delete them at any time from within the app.

Permissions

Franco does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, files, or any other sensitive data.

Third-Party Services

Franco does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Franco does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
